Leadership Team

Meet the administrative and operational heart of HERO

Overview

HERO has two branches: an administrative branch overseen by the Board of Directors and an operational branch overseen by the Operations Board. Together, the boards work to maintain the integrity of HERO's policy decisions, mission, and vision.

Captain: Betania Arce

Betania Arce is honored to be serving as the Captain for 2025. Betania is from Miami, Florida and is majoring in Biomedical Engineering (’26). She joined HERO as a prior EMT her freshman spring. As Captain, Betania helps oversee the operations of HERO. She has previously served as President and Secretary of HERO. Outside of HERO, Betania does research at Bayview and can be found enjoying a caffeinated beverage.
