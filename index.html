About HERO

HERO is a professional EMS organization operating on the Johns Hopkins Homewood Campus with support from Lifeline's Critical Care Team, Johns Hopkins Security, and the Student Health & Wellness Center. Our 24/7 volunteer EMTs provide free BLS-level care on over 300 calls annually.

Medical Emergency?

Call 410-516-7777 or use any blue light phone. HERO provides immediate, free emergency response to the Johns Hopkins community.

CPR Classes

Get certified through HERO! We offer American Heart Association CPR/AED courses for individuals and groups. Contact hero_cpr@jhu.edu or fill out our interest form.

Standby Requests

Need EMT coverage at your event? HERO supports Hopkins-affiliated activities such as Spring Fair, Alumni Weekend, and Commencement. Email hero_standby@jhu.edu to request standby service.

Amnesty Policy

HERO ensures confidentiality under Maryland EMS protocols. Johns Hopkins’ amnesty policy protects students who seek medical attention for themselves or others. Learn more here.

HERO License Number

HERO operates under an official Maryland EMS Organizational License, ensuring full compliance with state standards for training and emergency medical service.
